# GenerativeDefectProfile v1

Status: design specification  
Owner phase: v0.6 Repair & Stem Lab  
Related: #45, #50, #52, #54, #63

## Purpose

`GenerativeDefectProfileV1` is a versioned evidence contract for audible defects in generated music. It is independent from `AudioProfile`, CLaMP retrieval, analyzer-build `DRIFT`, mastering decisions and AI-origin detection.

It describes:

- what was measured;
- where a suspected defect occurs;
- which detector produced the evidence;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- whether repair is eligible;
- how a derived candidate changed defect evidence and musical-damage guards.

It must never claim that a track was generated by a particular service or model.

## Truth hierarchy

```text
immutable source
  -> measured features
  -> detector evidence
  -> reviewed defect annotation
  -> repair eligibility
  -> derived repair candidate
  -> before/after evidence + listening decision
```

Detector output is evidence, not ground truth. Human review is required for corpus labels and automatic winner promotion.

## Top-level schema

```json
{
  "schema": "generative-defect-profile/1",
  "profile_id": "gdp1:<sha256>:<analysis_identity>",
  "source": {},
  "analysis_identity": {},
  "global_qc": {},
  "markers": [],
  "repair_eligibility": [],
  "review": {},
  "extensions": {}
}
```

### `source`

Required:

- `asset_sha256`;
- `duration_s`;
- `sample_rate_hz`;
- `channels`;
- `bit_depth` when known;
- `codec/container`;
- `source_role`: `ORIGINAL_MIX | PROVIDED_STEM | ESTIMATED_STEM | DERIVED_REPAIR`;
- `parent_asset_sha256` for derived assets;
- `provenance_known`: boolean;
- optional user-supplied generator/service/model/version metadata.

The profile must not infer generator identity from audio.

### `analysis_identity`

Required:

- Genre_test version and Git commit;
- detector bundle revision;
- configuration hash;
- dependency/runtime identity;
- analysis timestamp;
- deterministic seed where applicable.

### `global_qc`

Objective fields shared with `TechnicalProfileOutputV1` where available:

- integrated loudness, loudness range and sample/true peak;
- peak-to-loudness ratio and crest summaries;
- clipped-sample/run counts;
- DC offset;
- bandwise mid/side energy and inter-channel correlation;
- mono-loss estimate;
- spectral cutoff/rolloff evidence;
- transient/onset summaries;
- decoded codec peak and codec-preview deltas when requested.

Fields must carry units, algorithm identity and availability/error state.

## Marker contract

Each marker is a bounded interval, not a track-wide adjective.

Required fields:

- `marker_id`;
- `start_s`, `end_s`;
- `defect_type`;
- `scope`: `FULL_MIX | VOCAL | DRUMS | BASS | OTHER | UNKNOWN_STEM`;
- `evidence[]`: metric name, value, units, baseline/reference, detector revision;
- `confidence`: `LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH`;
- `severity`: `NOTICE | MINOR | MAJOR | CRITICAL`;
- `review_state`: `UNREVIEWED | CONFIRMED | REJECTED | AMBIGUOUS`;
- `damage_class`;
- `recommended_routes[]`;
- `notes`.

Initial defect taxonomy:

- `METALLIC_HIGH_FREQUENCY_TEXTURE`;
- `SIBILANCE_OR_CONSONANT_DAMAGE`;
- `WARBLE_OR_ZIPPER_MODULATION`;
- `SPECTRAL_HOLE_OR_BANDWIDTH_COLLAPSE`;
- `CLICK_CRACKLE_DISCONTINUITY`;
- `CLIPPING_OR_FLAT_TOP`;
- `TRANSIENT_SMEAR_OR_ATTACK_LOSS`;
- `PHASE_SMEAR_OR_MONO_LOSS`;
- `LOW_LEVEL_SYNTHETIC_NOISE`;
- `STEM_BLEED`;
- `SEPARATION_MUSICAL_NOISE`;
- `CHUNK_BOUNDARY_DISCONTINUITY`;
- `GAIN_OR_LOUDNESS_ANOMALY`;
- `UNKNOWN_AUDIBLE_DEFECT`.

Taxonomy revisions require explicit schema/mapping changes. Fixed frequency cuts or genre-independent thresholds are not embedded as universal truth.

## Repair eligibility

Each route receives `RECOMMENDED | PROBE_ONLY | SKIP | HUMAN_REVIEW` plus reasons:

- `DSP_ONLY`;
- `LOCAL_INPAINT`;
- `SOURCE_RESTORATION`;
- `VOCAL_STEM`;
- `MULTI_STEM`;
- `REGENERATE_SOURCE`;
- `NO_ACTION`.

Eligibility considers defect class, source provenance, lossy/transcode evidence, stereo risk, transient risk, stem quality and model license/runtime availability.

## Before/after comparison

A comparison record links source and candidate by hashes and includes:

- time-alignment method and residual offset;
- loudness-match method and applied gain;
- marker count/severity delta;
- per-band spectral delta;
- transient attack retention;
- mono retention and phase stability;
- clipping/NaN/silence/gain guards;
- stem reconstruction residual where applicable;
- analyzer robustness results under `SOURCE_RESTORATION`;
- human ratings for artifact reduction and musical damage separately;
- verdict: `ACCEPT | REJECT | INCONCLUSIVE | REGENERATE_SOURCE`.

A lower marker count alone cannot select a winner.

## Separation safeguards

Estimated stems are derived evidence, never source truth. Record:

- separator code/model/checkpoint identity and license;
- stem hashes;
- mixture reconstruction residual;
- alignment/latency;
- model disagreement when multiple separators are tested;
- leakage/musical-noise markers;
- full-mix baseline result;
- explicit `FULL_MIX_WINS` verdict when separation causes more damage than benefit.

## Failure semantics

Every section supports:

- `OK`;
- `N/A` — detector/backend not applicable or absent;
- `WARN` — usable with incomplete/weak evidence;
- `FAIL` — configured operation failed;
- `UNKNOWN` — evidence insufficient.

Failure of this profile must not break ordinary Analyze or mutate `AudioProfile`.

## Privacy and boundary

- no AI-detector score target;
- no watermark/provenance removal target;
- no generator attribution from audio;
- no destructive source overwrite;
- user-provided private audio and annotations are excluded from Git;
- repository fixtures must be redistributable or synthetic; private real excerpts are referenced by hashes/manifests only.

## v1 acceptance

- JSON Schema and Pydantic model;
- deterministic serialization and identity hash;
- at least one positive and one negative fixture per promoted detector;
- reviewed marker inter-rater protocol;
- compatibility mapping to TechnicalProfile fields;
- before/after comparison schema;
- source/stem/derived lineage tests;
- ordinary v0.4/v0.5 outputs unchanged.
